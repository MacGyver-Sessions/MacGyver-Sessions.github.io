Are you presenting at a MacGyver session, or have you presented at one in the past? 

#### Use the MacGyver Logo
Include the [logo](https://macgyver-sessions.github.io/macgyver-logo) in your poster or presentation. 

#### Upload to Zenodo
Ready to share your work? Upload your poster, presentation or design to Zenodo, and submit it to the [MacGyver community](https://zenodo.org/communities/macgyver). You can also use [Zenodo's Github integration](https://help.zenodo.org/docs/github/) to upload a release of your Github repository. If your work already has a DOI elsewhere, you can incorporate it into the Zenodo repository.
